                Moisture on each day of drying, counted from unloading. One ferment is in colour: the one you picked, or the newest. The dashed line is your target, set on Answer choices.

                Drying places compared

                Where each ferment dried (the place used most in its drying checks), for ferments whose outside is known to be dry. Outside dry is the days from unloading to the first day the outside was dry: under 2 is too fast, 4 or more is too slow. Days to target count from unloading.

                Compare ferments

                Finished ferments grouped by plan, by shape (boxes and days) and by whether juice was taken. Yield is each group's dry weight ÷ wet weight.
